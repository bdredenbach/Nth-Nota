# Nth Nota 0.13.3 — the tour at your pace

Android preview version code 28.

The practice walkthrough is fully manual. Tutorial → Explore the tour, or Show me in a tool, opens its first prepared example without starting animated playback. Next demonstrates one step and stays there until you choose another. Back and the chapter picker remain available. The pace control changes demonstration animation speed only.

There is no Play/Pause button, automatic playback setting, or timed step advancement. Try it lets you take over the real practice controls; Back to tour restores the full guide without rerunning the example or discarding practice edits. Interacting with a demonstration also hands control to you. Next waits for a demonstration to finish, while Try it can interrupt it. A failed example offers Retry step in place of Next.

All 25 chapters, prepared examples, manual tips, reduced motion, Android Back, and restoration of the original draft remain. Practice changes stay out of saved notes.

Browser checks cover manual navigation, a step staying in place beyond the previous automatic interval, chapter selection, practice and return, narrow-screen controls, all real example steps, and restoration of the original draft and companion.
