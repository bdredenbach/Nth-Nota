# Nth Nota 0.8.0 — canvas, audio and useful examples

- Every app launch starts in Canvas with the Desk menu. Theme, notes and other
  preferences are preserved. List and sidebar remain available during a session.
- The bottom taskbar switches Canvas/List and announces the chosen view in a
  toast. Settings and Workspace view use the same behavior.
- Snap to grid, beside the canvas status, arranges visible open notes in a
  left-aligned column. It stays in Canvas; notes remain independently movable.
  Closed, minimized, deleted and filtered-out notes are not moved.
- Audio cards offer Convert to text at the bottom center. A saved transcript
  is displayed and editable inside the same audio note, and previewed on its
  canvas card. Conversion no longer creates a second note. Audio and transcript
  stay together in backups, and transcript text is searchable.
- New desks have six editable examples in each of Everyday, Projects and Ideas.
  Projects includes a checklist, brief, meeting notes, budget, sketch and audio.
  Ideas includes writing prompts, a collection, a story, experiment tasks, a
  visual idea map and a comparison. Existing desks gain missing Projects/Ideas
  examples once; edits, closed/deleted examples and personal notes are preserved.
  Empty desks stay empty. Explicit backup restoration does not add examples.
- The user-supplied Nastelbom/Pixabay track is represented by a 30-second faded
  preview as part of a project-audio example. The original upload is unchanged.
  Source, filename, hashes, creator and license links are retained in the asset
  credit record. The music sample is labeled as music; transcription waits for
  an explicit action rather than automatically treating it as spoken dictation.

The existing microphone, local transcription, AI consent and reporting paths
remain in use. This update requires an APK/web-shell update, not a Worker change.
Actual speech recognition quality and native notification delivery still need
device testing. Automated browser checks use controlled native bridge fixtures.
