Both the short tool tips and the practice tutor use translucent backgrounds. The page remains interactive beneath the panel. Drag the three-line grip with a finger, pen or mouse; keyboard arrows move a focused grip, Shift makes larger moves, and Home returns to automatic positioning. Moving the panel preserves its chosen location between interactions and keeps it inside the visible viewport when the phone resizes.

The practice tour begins with a slide explaining the grip and green double circle. Each writing interaction starts blank and shows an optional example outside the field. Type your own words, then choose Next. For button interactions, tap the marked control or use Next to activate it. Choices and drawings also stay under your control. There is no timed advancement or automatic typing. Back replays only your own earlier entries so they remain available. Try it can hide the explanation; Back to tour brings the current instructions back.

The target marker follows each requested field and control, remains visible with reduced motion, and never intercepts input. The short tips are nonmodal and no longer trap keyboard focus. They also suggest wording for the highlighted entry fields.

New entries no longer insert starter wording into titles, reminder messages, mind-map labels and connections, math rows, budget category names, option names, snippets, references and routine names. Generated card names remain available as placeholders; an authored title with the same wording is preserved. Existing notes are not cleared. Dates, numeric settings and schema definitions retain their useful defaults.

Practice data stays in its temporary desk. Exiting restores the original editor, draft, companion and stored desk. Prepared source pictures, audio and companion replies remain labeled examples; real permissions, recordings, AI requests, backup replacement and exports require leaving practice.

Verification exercises every chapter through real user input and clicks, blank starting fields, personal wording through Back, a real touch drag, keyboard movement, viewport clamping, target positions, manual tips with live writing, original-draft restoration and browser offline use.
