# Nth Nota 0.8.2

The canvas footer now offers Delete workspace beside Snap to grid. Cancel has initial focus. Confirm removes only the named workspace and moves its open, minimized and saved notes to Recently deleted. Notes, attachments, transcripts and history remain recoverable in the first remaining workspace; deleted-note reminders are no longer scheduled. Previously trashed notes keep their deletion dates. Deleting the final workspace creates an empty Everyday replacement without reintroducing samples.

The redundant Saved notes shelf below the canvas is removed. The dock appears only for minimized notes. Saved notes remains available in the bottom taskbar and Desk menu. Workspace tabs above the canvas remain; workspace shortcuts are removed from the Desk menu.

Three creation tools are added to Desk: Handwrite to text, Meeting note and Idea starter. The latter two are editable note templates that open on the active workspace. Existing sketch/ink remains drawing rather than text recognition. The editor creation ribbon also offers handwriting conversion.

Handwrite to text records primary finger or pen strokes and converts a word or short line through Google ML Kit Digital Ink Recognition 19.0.0 on Android. First use explicitly initiated by Convert downloads a language model, approximately 20 MB. Download and recognition status are indeterminate rather than invented percentages. Users review/edit the reading or choose an alternative, add words and continue. Save to workspace also accepts the reviewed pending words, preserving one original note. Strokes, pending readings and accepted text persist through closing, backups and reload. Continue typing opens the regular editor. A browser can keep the draft but requires the Android app for conversion.

Stop detaches the result and keeps the draft. A model download managed by the SDK can finish in the background; this is stated in the UI. Android pause/destroy cancels the request, and recognizers close only when their inference task completes. Payloads are bounded to 512 strokes / 12,000 points, validated on both sides of the bridge and during backup restore. Extra fingers and cancelled pointer strokes do not become writing. Recognition quality, first download, offline second use and pause/resume still need a physical-device test; browser fixtures do not perform inference.

The bundled privacy policy and Worker privacy HTML disclose handwriting downloads and Google's ML Kit diagnostic/usage metrics. The writing/report endpoint logic, models, quotas and credentials are unchanged. Deploy the updated `cloud/worker.mjs` to refresh the publicly hosted privacy page; the APK uses its updated bundled copy independently. Consult Google's terms and data disclosure when completing Play Data safety:

- https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/vision/digital-ink-recognition/android
- https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms
- https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure
